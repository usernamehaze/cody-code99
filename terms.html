Cody: Terms

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Cody is a free study helper made by a student team for the Code 99: CSS Hackathon 2027. By using Cody you agree to these terms. Please also read our Privacy page.

Using Cody

  • Cody is for studying: making reviewers, practising and asking questions about your lessons.
  • Use it to learn, not to cheat. Follow your school's rules, and don't use Cody during a test or exam unless your teacher allows it.
  • Only upload notes, photos and files you are allowed to use.

AI can make mistakes

Cody's reviewers, quizzes, drawings and answers are made by AI. They can be wrong or leave something out. Check them against your own notes and your teacher before you rely on them.

Class rooms

  • Be kind. No insults, bullying, rude words, or anything not fit for a classroom.
  • Only share reviewers and drawings you made or may share.
  • Whatever you post in a class room is seen by everyone in that class.

Your account and Premium

  • Signing in with Google is optional. You can delete your account at any time in Me → Your data.
  • Premium (Make it yours and Reviewer mastery) is free during Code 99. If it ever costs money, we'll say so clearly first, and nothing will be charged without your choice.

Help when you need it

Cody is a study buddy, not a doctor or counsellor. If you are going through a hard time, talk to someone you trust, or call the NCMH Crisis Hotline: 1553 (free, any time, day or night).

No guarantees

Cody is a student project, offered as it is. It may change, have bugs, or be unavailable at times, and we can't promise your results in school.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes too.

Contact

Open an issue on Cody's public GitHub page: github.com/usernamehaze/cody-code99/issues. Don't post anything private there; ask us for another way to reach you.
